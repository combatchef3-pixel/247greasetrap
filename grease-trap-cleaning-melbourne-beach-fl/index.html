Frequently Asked Questions

How often should our grease trap be serviced?

While it varies by volume, most commercial kitchens require service every 30 to 90 days. We recommend a schedule that ensures the grease and solids never exceed 25% of the trap's capacity to avoid backups and odors.

Does your service include the disposal of the collected grease?

Yes, we handle the entire process. We pump the waste, transport it to a licensed facility, and ensure it is disposed of or recycled in full compliance with environmental laws.

Can you service our hoods and grease traps at the same time?

Absolutely. We specialize in comprehensive kitchen maintenance and can coordinate both services to minimize the number of visits and disruptions to your kitchen.

What happens if we fail a city inspection?

If you have a compliance issue, contact us immediately. We can perform an emergency clean-out and provide the necessary documentation to help you resolve the violation quickly.

Do you offer service for national accounts with multiple locations?

Yes, we specialize in national account management, providing centralized billing, standardized reporting, and a single point of contact for all your locations across the U.S.
